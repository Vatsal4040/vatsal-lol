# CHAOS: The Internet's Most Beautiful Sound Playground
## 06. Anti-Scope & Do-Not-Build Checklist

To prevent feature creep and ensure CHAOS retains its playful, minimalist toy-box essence, the application must never implement or incorporate any of the following features. If a future request asks for one of these, it must be rejected.

---

## 1. Things CHAOS Must Never Become

* **❌ Spotify:** Do not build song playlists, music sharing systems, track queueing managers, album categorization, or track search inputs.
* **❌ Meditation App:** Do not add breathing timers, guided voices, visual relaxations, ambient video backdrops, or journal sections.
* **❌ White Noise Utility:** Do not structure UI around scientific color noises (Brownian, White, Pink) or focus/sleep ratings.
* **❌ Music Player:** Do not implement equalizer controls, stereo panning settings, audio pitch-shifters, or speed controls.
* **❌ System Dashboard:** Do not use analytical tables, charts, grids of toggle statistics, or system status graphs.
* **❌ Productivity Tool:** Do not add Pomodoro clocks, task lists, calendar syncs, integrations, or timer popups.
* **❌ Settings-Heavy App:** Do not build multi-tab settings panes, dropdown configs, audio latency adjustment menus, or UI customizers.
* **❌ Social Network:** Do not include comments, sound mix rating stars, user profiles, following lists, or user messaging.
* **❌ AI Chatbot / Assistant:** Do not place chat boxes, voice control, automated mix assistants, or smart recommendations.
* **❌ Login / Identity System:** Do not build sign-up forms, email verifications, passwords, OAuth integrations, or user databases.
* **❌ Gamified Platform:** Do not force achievements, badges, level-up milestones, unlock streaks, or daily check-in rewards.

---

## 2. Core Rule
Keep the application simple, playful, fast, and beautiful. If a proposed feature does not make the sensory act of mixing sounds more enjoyable, **do not build it.**
