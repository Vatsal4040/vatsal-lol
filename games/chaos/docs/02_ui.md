# CHAOS: The Internet's Most Beautiful Sound Playground
## 02. User Interface & Experience Specification

---

## 2.1 Visual Style Constraints
The layout simulates a hand-drawn, sticker-pack board.
* **Background:** Almost black, dark charcoal gray (`#121212` or `#161616`).
* **Cards & Panels:** Slightly lighter charcoal gray (`#1e1e1e` or `#222222`).
* **Borders:** Thick wobbly/uneven borders (`border-width: 2px`, solid white `#ffffff` or dark charcoal `#333333`).
* **Text / Sketch Color:** Warm white or chalky gray (`#e5e5e5`).
* **Shadows:** No smooth modern box-shadow blurs. If shadow is used, it must be a hard, offset flat shadow (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000`).
* **Restrictions:**
  * Maximum `border-radius`: `18px`.
  * No linear or radial background gradients.
  * No glowing neon outer rings or glossy reflective buttons.
  * No glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter`) or transparency blurs.

---

## 2.2 Typography
We want a warm, storybook, sketch-like typography to match the hand-drawn elements.
* **Primary Heading / Logo Font:** `Schoolbell` or `Patrick Hand` (Google Fonts), fallback to `Comic Sans MS`.
* **Body / Card Font:** `Short Stack` or `Gochi Hand` (Google Fonts), fallback to `cursive`/`sans-serif`.
* **Title Hierarchy:** Large, chunky, handwritten titles with slight tilt.

---

## 2.3 UI States for Sound Library Cards
Each of the 30 sound cards in the grid operates in one of four visual states:

1. **Idle State:**
   * Normal card styling.
   * Grayscale/desaturated mascot icon.
   * Hard wobbly border.
2. **Hover State:**
   * Lift card by `4px` using transforms (`transform: translateY(-4px) rotate(random_tilt)`).
   * Hover transition duration: `120ms`.
3. **Playing State:**
   * Mascot icon saturates to full color.
   * Soft, flat outer glow behind the card.
   * Animated particles float up from the card.
   * Tiny playing indicator/badge becomes visible on the card.
4. **Muted State:**
   * Card is set to `50%` opacity.
   * Icon remains saturated but faded.
   * Particles stop spawning.

---

## 2.4 Sound Card Specification (Sticker Collection Aesthetic)
To make the page feel like a collection of interactive stickers rather than a soundboard panel:
* **Whole-Card Clickable:** Clicking anywhere on the card toggles the sound. There are **no individual play/pause buttons** on the grid cards.
* **Card Contents:** Mascot icon, Sound name, and a tiny active indicator badge (glows or pulses when playing).
* **Grid Stability:** Cards must be exactly the same size. No card should grow or shrink in physical width/height upon hover, activation, or muting. Animations must use `transform` overlays or opacity changes, ensuring cards never shift surrounding grid items.
* **Wobbly Border Effect:** Achieved using uneven CSS border-radius curves:
  `border-radius: 255px 15px 225px 15px / 15px 225px 15px 255px;`
* **Random Rotation:** Cards are initialized with a slight random tilt (between `-1.5deg` and `1.5deg`) to create an organic, scattered sticker-book appearance.

---

## 2.5 Now Playing Behavior
The Now Playing sidebar displays only the currently active sounds.
* **No sorting:** When a sound starts, append its control card to the bottom of the active stack. Preserve the user's exact activation order.
* **Minimalist controls:** Each card contains only the Mascot icon, Sound name, hand-drawn volume slider, mute/unmute button, and a remove (X) button.
* **Non-destructive removal:** Removing a sound via the Now Playing list stops only that sound. It must not reset or affect the volumes of other active sounds.
* **No DOM thrashing:** Adding or removing a sound card must only insert or delete that specific DOM element. The entire Now Playing list must never be completely cleared and rebuilt from scratch on card changes.

---

## 2.6 Hand-Drawn Sliders
Both the individual sound sliders and the bottom Master Volume slider share a custom sketch-like look:
* **Track:** Thick dark-grey border with wobbly border-radius.
* **Fill:** Solid white.
* **Knob:** Large white circular thumb that slowly wiggles or performs a gentle "breathing" pulse animation when idle.
* **Drag State:** When a user drags the knob, the track and thumb become slightly thicker.

---

## 2.7 Preset Interaction Behavior
* **Activation Flow:** When a user clicks a Preset card:
  1. Fade out and stop every active sound that is *not* in the target preset.
  2. Wait `200ms` for the fade-out to complete.
  3. Load the preset sounds.
  4. Fade all preset sounds in together.
  5. Update the Now Playing list dynamically.
  6. Animate the Chaos Meter to the new level.
* **Caching Protection:** Do not clear or reload audio files that are already loaded in memory. If a sound is already playing and is also in the preset, simply adjust its volume level smoothly without resetting playback.

---

## 2.8 Minimalist Settings Panel
The Settings menu is extremely simple and supports only the following controls:
* **Master Volume:** Permanent volume level multiplier slider.
* **Reduce Motion Toggle:** Disables wiggles, bouncing cards, and floating particle effects for performance or accessibility.
* **Reset Discoveries:** Clears unlocked hidden presets from `localStorage` and resets them to locked state.
* **About:** Short hand-sketched credits block.

---

## 2.9 Interface Layout
The screen layout is optimized for a single-screen viewport:
* **Top Header:** Hand-drawn logo, Chaos Meter wiggling dynamically, and a settings/info icon at the top right.
* **Center Left - Sound Library Grid:** 30 cards representing the sound sandbox. No search bar.
* **Center Right - Now Playing Sidebar:** Stack of active sound controllers. Appears only after the first sound is clicked.
* **Bottom - Chaos Presets Row:** Scrollable shelf of preset cards. Reveals itself with a fade-in animation 10 seconds after the user's first interaction.
* **Fixed Bottom Bar - Master Volume:** Permanent master slider at the bottom.

---

## 2.10 Mobile Layout (Stacked Flow)
On smaller screens, layout adapts to prevent horizontal scrolling:
* Header (Logo & Chaos Meter) stacks at the top.
* Sound grid scales down to 2 columns.
* Now Playing controllers stack vertically below the grid.
* Presets shelf converts to a swipeable carousel.
* Master Volume is fixed to the bottom of the viewport.

---

## 2.11 Animation & Performance Budgets

### Animation Budget
* **Maximum Animation Duration:** `300ms`.
* **Maximum Simultaneous Active Animations:** `3`.
* **Properties Allowed to Animate:** `opacity`, `transform` (translate, scale, rotate), `filter`.
* **Properties Banned from Animating:** `width`, `height`, `top`, `left`, `margin`, `padding`.

### Performance Budget
* **First Paint:** `< 1 second`.
* **Interaction Delay:** `< 50ms`.
* **Animations Frame Rate:** Smooth `60fps`.
* **Reflow Prevention:** Perform all DOM reads before writes.
* **Lazy Loading:** Audio loops are loaded on-demand when clicked.
* **Object Reuse:** Reuse Audio objects in a pool.
* **Event Listeners:** Prevent duplicate listeners. Maintain single references.
